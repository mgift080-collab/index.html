📚 Money Guides

Simple explanations to help you understand common money calculations.

How to Calculate Profit

Profit is the amount left after subtracting the cost of something from its selling price.

Formula: Profit = Selling Price − Cost Price.

For example, if a product costs R500 and you sell it for R800, the gross profit is R300.

How to Build a Savings Goal

Start by choosing a specific amount you want to save and a deadline.

  • Choose your target amount.
  • Subtract money you already have saved.
  • Divide the remaining amount by the number of months available.
  • Review your budget regularly.

What Is Compound Growth?

Compound growth happens when returns are added to an investment and future returns can then be earned on the increased balance.

Actual investment returns vary and are never guaranteed. The calculator on this website is only an estimate.

How to Start a Side Hustle

A side hustle works best when it solves a real problem for a specific group of people.

  • Choose a skill or problem you understand.
  • Research whether people actually need it.
  • Start with a small version.
  • Keep your costs low.
  • Improve based on real customer feedback.
